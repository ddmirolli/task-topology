# Customer-local due dates

An unpaid invoice becomes overdue on the calendar day after its due date in
the customer's timezone. Keep that rule consistent in invoice lists, the
overdue filter, and reminder emails. A paid or deleted invoice must not receive
an overdue reminder. Use UTC when the customer's timezone is missing.

Handle dates around midnight, month and year boundaries, and daylight saving
changes. Do not hard-code Australia or the server's timezone. Existing callers
of the reminder job and the controllable app clock must keep working.
