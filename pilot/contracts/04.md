# Customer deletion

After deleting a customer, remove them from the active customer list and keep
their invoices and line items. Preserve invoice numbers, dates, amounts,
payment status, and the customer name shown on old invoices. Other customers
and their invoices must be unchanged. Keep the invoice page and invoice API
usable after deletion. The confirmation must not say invoices will be deleted.

This must work with existing databases as well as new ones, including after
the app restarts. A soft deletion or a data-preserving schema change is allowed.
No particular migration file or database technique is required.
