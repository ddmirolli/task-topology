1. `due_on` is a date. `lib/overdue.js` compares it to `new Date()` in
   server local time. Fix is compare on calendar date in the customer's
   timezone, which exists on the customer row.
2. `jobs/remind-overdue.js` has its own copy of the comparison.
3. A customer with no timezone set must not crash.
