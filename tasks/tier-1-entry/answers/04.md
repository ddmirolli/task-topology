# 04 answers

Gotchas:
1. `ON DELETE CASCADE` is in `db/schema.sql`. SQLite needs a migration
   that rebuilds the table. Editing schema.sql alone does nothing to
   existing databases.
2. `views/customers/confirm-delete.ejs` says "and all of their invoices".
   The copy must change.
3. Invoices must keep a readable customer name after the customer row is
   gone, or be reassigned to a placeholder. Either passes. Orphaned
   invoices that crash the invoices page fail.
