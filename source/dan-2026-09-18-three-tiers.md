# Dan, 2026-09-18, on the three task tiers

Dan authorized project-name normalization on 2026-09-19. This excerpt uses the
approved brand and domain; it is not a verbatim transcript. Other wording is
unchanged. Preserve that wording when using it for the site or README.

> The goal is that this, as a standard, could be open source but also housed and displayed at something like modeltopography.com. So as far, or maybe that's the wrong way to think about this, and we would just display the results at modeltopography.com and then link to the open source GitHub repo. Where people could download it and run it themselves in their own harness. As far as the tickets, I'm wondering if the best way to do the tickets is actually to have three different versions of tasks that are standardized across all models. One that is like quick but complex, like there are a few gotchas in the task, so that models that don't have a good sense of intent would skip over or not be able to read in between the lines, therefore betray low EQ and IQ and thus fail. But if they Are above a certain IQ threshold, they would obviously understand the intent and implication behind the task, and therefore be able to accomplish it incredibly quickly. So that would be like the first style task. The next style would be like a complex style ticket or a Jira ticket or something like that that involves. Checking multiple databases and reconciling them, and then reporting back. So think multi-gated access, multi-database access, standardization of data across those different disparate data sets, and then coming back with a summary, a conclusion, and a recommended action, and. That that would be judged based off of completeness, accuracy of results, and the clarity of the message, judging a model's ability to communicate clearly and effectively. And then the third one would be a truly like multi-generational run of hey, I need you to stand up a ticketing system. That deploys inside of our platform to replace Jira, something like that. That is a simple but unbelievably complex and wide breadth task that would involve a shit ton of work off of a single prompt. And you could basically judge these as far as like a label, you could. Parse these and label them based off of hierarchy of human employees. So the first task that I mention would be an entry level job. A the middle task that I mention would be a mid level management job. The third task that I mention would be a senior executive job. And so, based off of all three of those specific roles and the corresponding tasks that are involved, that's actually a really good proxy for answering the question: Which model should I use here? Because it's a proxy of agency, which is sort of a linguistic composite of intelligence. Lateral thinking and capability, and capability being a combination of effectiveness, effectiveness and efficiency. And so I think that might be the best way to do the quote unquote tickets, and then on the results that display. The actual Model Topography Benchmark, the MTB, we would be able to have selections to show the plotted graph of any model that it has run MTB across entry level, middle management, and senior executive.
